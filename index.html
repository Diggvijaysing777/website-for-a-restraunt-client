About Us

Paragon — an epitome of perfection

Hotel Grand Paragon Palace has welcomed guests to Solan since 2002. Spread across roughly 60,000 square feet in the heart of the town, the property draws its character from royal heritage architecture — arched frontages, generous proportions and detailing that belongs to an older Himachal — while the rooms, restaurant and banquets behind that frontage are thoroughly modern.

The name says what the house aims at. Our team of thirty looks after comfortable accommodation, warm hospitality, dining through the day, and celebrations that range from an intimate ring ceremony to a wedding of more than a thousand guests — all of it a short walk from The Mall, with the valley in view.

Explore Solan

Step out and you are already there

Being on The Mall means the town is on foot and the hills are a short drive. Travel times below are approximate.

  • 9 minwalk

    Solan Railway Station

    The narrow-gauge Kalka–Shimla line, and a pleasant stroll to reach it.

  • 8 mindrive

    Maa Shoolini Temple

    The temple the town is named for, and the centre of the Shoolini Mela.

  • ~2 kmaway

    Mohan Shakti Heritage Park

    Stone-carved pavilions and gardens laid out across the hillside.

  • NearbySolan

    Jatoli Shiv Mandir

    A towering South-Indian-style shikhara, said to be among the highest in Asia.

  • Day tripDagshai

    Dagshai Jail Museum

    A colonial-era cantonment prison, now a small and sobering museum.

  • Day tripKasauli

    Timber Trail & Kasauli

    Cable car over the gorge, then the cobbled quiet of a colonial hill town.
